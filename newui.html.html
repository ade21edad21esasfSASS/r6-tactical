🧠 Map Knowledge & Tactics

Learn the Maps

Room names appear on your HUD – use them for callouts. Study blueprints on the Ubisoft website to see soft walls, hatches, and destructible floors. Download floorplans and label them for a second-monitor cheat sheet.

🎯 Modernized maps: Emerald Plains, Kanal, Outback (2026)

Jiggle Peeking & Clearing

Strafe and lean around a corner, then immediately return to cover – exposes minimal hitbox while gathering intel. When clearing a room, peek one angle at a time, from left-to-right or right-to-left depending on your door position.

Destruction is a Tool

Turn walls into windows. Use Buck or Sledge to open unexpected sightlines. On defense, create rotates that support plant denial – don't copy old reinforce patterns blindly.

Audio & Positioning

Reverb and propagation updates help pinpoint enemy location. Use drones to confirm pathing before committing. Assign a flank watch before executing the plant – patience wins rounds.
